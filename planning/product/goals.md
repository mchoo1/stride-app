# Stride — Product Goals & Success Metrics
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## North Star

Get Singapore residents to use Stride as the first thing they check before ordering food — making Stride the default macro reference for SG dining, the way Google Maps is the default for directions.

---

## Primary Goal

**Become Singapore's go-to macro food search engine.**

Stride wins when users open the app not just to log what they ate, but to decide what to eat — because Stride has the most complete, most accurate SG nutrition data of any tool available.

---

## Product Goals by Phase

### Phase 1 — Establish (Now)
> Build the most complete, accurate SG macro database and make it instantly searchable.

- Cover all major SG fast food chains with official verified nutrition data
- Cover key hawker dishes from all major hawker centres
- Search returns relevant results within 2 seconds
- Users can filter by calories, protein, price, and dietary need simultaneously
- First-time users understand the value (SG-specific data) within 60 seconds of opening the app

### Phase 2 — Grow (6–12 months)
> Get users to return daily by combining food discovery with tracking.

- Daily active use: users log at least one meal per day
- Expand database to cover all popular SG food categories (bakeries, cafes, bubble tea, convenience stores)
- Community submissions begin filling gaps — verified crowd-sourced data
- Wearable sync closes the energy balance loop (calories in vs. out)
- GrabFood / Foodpanda integration — users see macros before ordering delivery

### Phase 3 — Monetise (12–24 months)
> Build a sustainable business on top of the data and user base.

- Stride Pro subscription — advanced features for serious fitness users
- Restaurant and brand partnerships — verified data listing + promoted placement
- Activity marketplace — class booking, PT sessions, gym memberships
- Affiliate revenue from delivery app deep-links

## User Goals (What Users Want)

| User Type | Primary Goal | How Stride Helps |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Gym-goer | Hit daily protein target | Search by protein, see grams per item, log with one tap |
| Weight loss | Stay under calorie budget | Remaining calories shown on Eat page, filter by kcal |
| Busy professional | Make a fast, smart food choice | Search restaurant + dietary need → ranked results in seconds |
| Health-conscious | Know what's actually in SG food | Verified SG data, not US approximations |
| Fitness beginner | Understand macros without a plan | Onboarding sets targets automatically based on goal and stats |

## Key Metrics to Track

### Acquisition
- New user registrations per week
- Source breakdown (social, organic search, referral, direct)
- Conversion rate: landing page visit → registration

### Activation
- % of new users who complete onboarding (5-step wizard)
- % of new users who log their first meal within 24 hours
- % of new users who use food search (Eat page) within first session

### Retention
- Day 7 and Day 30 retention rates
- Daily streak distribution (how many users maintain 3-day, 7-day, 30-day streaks)
- Average sessions per week per active user

### Engagement
- Most-searched food items and restaurants (signals where to expand the database)
- Meal feedback submissions (signals data quality issues)
- Food log entries per user per day

### Data Quality
- % of food items with verified source (`official_sg`, `hpb`)
- Feedback submissions flagging incorrect macros
- Community food submissions pending review
