## Positioning Summary

**Stride is Singapore's macro food search engine.** Users search verified nutrition data across 30+ SG restaurants and hawker centres to find the best meals for their goals — and track calories, weight, water, and activity in one free app.

**Core angle: Eat Smart** — know what's in your food before you order.

**Live:** https://stride-app-rosy.vercel.app · **Contact:** stride.singapore@gmail.com
