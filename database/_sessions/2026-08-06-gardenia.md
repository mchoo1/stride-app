## Sourcing notes

All 18 items sourced directly from each product's own page on **gardenia.com.sg** (e.g. `gardenia.com.sg/gardenia-enriched-white-bread-400g/`), which reproduces the manufacturer's printed "Nutritional information" panel (Per Serving + Per 100g columns) exactly as required by SFA food labelling regulations. This is Singapore's largest bread manufacturer (QAF Limited; 60+ SKUs, 200,000+ loaves baked daily), so these are near-universally available at every SG supermarket, convenience store, and provision shop — high value for the app's grocery/staples coverage.

**Prices:** Where the exact SKU is sold on fairprice.com.sg (checked via `fairprice.com.sg/brand/gardenia`, 15 products listed), the live FairPrice price was used. For SKUs not listed there (specialty/gourmet loaves, wraps not in the 15-item brand page, individual buns/rolls), an approximate SG retail price was estimated from comparable Gardenia SKUs and flagged in `nutritionNote`.

**Halal tagging:** FairPrice's product listing shows an explicit "•Halal" badge per SKU (reflecting MUIS certification status), which is a reliable, product-specific official signal. `halal` was only added to `compatibleWith` for the 8 items with an unambiguous exact-name-and-size match to a Halal-badged FairPrice SKU. Notably, **Gardenia Loaf – Nutri Multi-Grain** and **Gardenia Loaf – California Raisin** are both listed on FairPrice *without* the Halal badge, so `halal` was deliberately left off those two even though they're the same brand. All other items (specialty loaves, wraps, buns, cream roll — not sold on fairprice.com.sg) were left with no halal tag since certification could not be confirmed per rules.

**Vegetarian/vegan:** All 18 items are vegetarian (no meat) except **Sambal Ikan Bilis Bun**, which contains ikan bilis (anchovies) and shrimp paste in the filling — excluded from `vegetarian`. No items are vegan (all contain milk powder, butter, or egg powder per their ingredient lists).

**Lactose-free:** Only the Sambal Ikan Bilis Bun has no dairy ingredient in its ingredient list; every other item contains skimmed milk powder, milk powder, or butter.

**Data-quality note:** The Gardenia Original Wraps label rendered sodium as "368g" per serving — this is clearly a typo for 368mg (consistent with the Per-100g value of 817mg for a 45g serving); corrected to 368mg in the entry below.
