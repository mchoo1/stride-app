## Why PlayMade

PlayMade is a Taiwanese bubble tea chain with a genuine Singapore outpost (`playmade.com`, HQ'd in Singapore under Cray Ventures Pte Ltd, +65 6254 1472, 6 Kallang-area registered). Unlike every other bubble tea / fast-casual candidate checked this session (Each-A-Cup, Tiger Sugar, The Alley, Seoul Garden, Sakae Sushi, Pepper Lunch, Fish & Co, Poulet, Swensen's, Ichiban Boshi, Kopi Kenangan — see "Outlets ruled out" below), PlayMade publishes an **actual nutrition-facts PDF** for its Singapore menu, complete with the mandatory Singapore **Nutri-Grade** rating column (A–D), at:

- Source page: https://www.playmade.com/nutritional-facts
- Source PDF: https://www.playmade.com/_files/ugd/b8185a_58ccdc1aa1b84b28a2e3b238754e4986.pdf

This is a real regulatory-grade document (Nutri-Grade labelling is mandatory in Singapore for pre-packaged and F&B beverages since Dec 2023), so the macro *ratios* are as official as it gets for a bubble tea chain.

## ⚠️ The one open question: cup volume

The PDF states values as "Energy (Kcal), Protein (g), Fat (g)... per [row]" without stating a serving weight/volume on the table itself — cross-checking the numbers against Singapore's Nutri-Grade grading thresholds (which are defined **per 100ml**) confirms the table is **per 100ml**, not per cup. The page footer notes "Calorie count is based on medium cup size and 100% sweetness, with regular ice" — describing how the recipe ratios were calculated, not the reporting unit.

PlayMade does not publish its cup capacity anywhere on its own site or app store listings. The one concrete figure found is **484ml**, cited for PlayMade's medium cup in a Yahoo SG Finance bubble-tea brand comparison article (aggregated, not primary). I used this to convert per-100ml → per-cup (×4.84) for every item below.

**This is why the whole outlet is filed as `needs_approval`, not `pending_upload`**, even though the underlying macro *ratios* are genuinely official. If you (admin) can confirm PlayMade's actual cup volume in ml (menu board, receipt, or asking a store), the per-cup totals should be rescaled by the correction factor and the outlet can move straight to `pending_upload` — no re-research needed, just multiply by (actual_ml / 484).

## Outlets ruled out this session (no usable official/HPB data found)

- **Seoul Garden** (Korean BBQ hot pot, explicitly flagged as a priority target in the task brief) — no published nutrition anywhere; only third-party menu-price sites.
- **Each-A-Cup** (bubble tea) — no official nutrition; only generic "bubble tea average" figures from third parties.
- **Tiger Sugar** — brand explicitly does not publish nutrition charts (confirmed via multiple sources); only third-party calorie estimates.
- **The Alley** — the one PDF found under its name is a third-party blog aggregation ("Talk Boba"), not an Alley-published document; no genuine official source located.
- **Sakae Sushi** — no SG-specific official nutrition; only FatSecret/MyFitnessPal crowd entries.
- **Pepper Lunch** — no official nutrition page; only FatSecret aggregation.
- **Fish & Co** — has an HPB Healthier Dining Programme angle (11 dishes reportedly under 500kcal) but the only source is a 2014 blog post; too old to trust against a menu that's likely changed, and not a live official list.
- **Poulet** — no official nutrition found.
- **Swensen's** — has official SG menu PDFs (multiple, current to 2026-03) but they are price/menu only, no macros.
- **Ichiban Boshi** — official site menu is images only, no calorie data found anywhere.
- **Kopi Kenangan / Kenangan Coffee SG** — official SG menu page has no calories; only 8 signature drinks listed with no nutrition; scattered third-party FatSecret entries only.
- **Pacific Coffee (SG)** — only generic HK/global FatSecret-style entries, nothing SG-specific or official.

Given this, PlayMade was the standout: a real brand-published Nutri-Grade document is much stronger footing than anything else checked.
