## Diet tag reasoning

- `high_protein` (protein ≥ 25g): all 6 bowls qualify — applied.
- `low_fat` (fat ≤ 10g): none qualify — not applied.
- `keto` (carbs ≤ 10g): none qualify — not applied.
- `vegetarian` / `vegan`: none of the 6 bowls are meat/fish-free (Rasa Nusantara contains sambal fish despite the tempeh; all others contain chicken/beef/tofu combos with animal protein) — not applied.
- `halal`: not confirmed from an official MUIS-certification source — left empty per rules.
- `lactose_free`: Genki Teriyaki Bowl's page explicitly lists "Contains Gluten, Dairy, and Nut Products," so not lactose-free. Dairy status for the other 5 bowls was not explicitly confirmed either way — left empty per rules (conservative).
