## Source

Mr Bean publishes a full Singapore Nutrition Information panel (Energy, Protein, Total Fat, Saturated Fat, Trans Fat, Cholesterol, Carbohydrate, Sugars, Dietary Fibre, Sodium, and occasionally Calcium) directly on each product's page at `https://www.mrbean.com.sg/product-info/[slug]`. These are the brand's own official per-serving/per-package figures, not third-party estimates.

The site's `/productinfos` listing page is client-rendered (JS), so it couldn't be scraped directly for a full URL list — individual product-info URLs were instead located via targeted web searches and fetched one at a time. This yielded 23 products with full nutrition panels across 9 of Mr Bean's ~13 menu categories: Soya Milk, Grass Jelly, Soya Ice Cream (incl. the Mr Bean x Udders collab line), Beancurd, Eggwich, Pancakes, Riceball, Millet Grains Soy Porridge, and Wholegrain Mixed Rice Bowl. Categories not covered this session (no product-info page located): Pouch Soya Milk, Soy Burger, Granola (bar range — one page found but had no nutrition table, only ingredients), Grab & Go desserts (grass jelly/dessert bowls).

**Halal:** Mr Bean's full Singapore outlet network (64 stores) became MUIS halal-certified in June 2022, confirmed via Mothership, HalalTrip, Goody Feed, and Singapore Women's Weekly reporting. Applied `halal` at the outlet level and to all core menu items. The one exception is the "Original Soy Ice Cream (Cup)" — a Mr Bean x Udders collaboration product — where halal status wasn't separately confirmed for the sub-brand, so the tag was withheld on that item specifically.

**Pricing:** mrbean.com.sg product-info pages don't list prices. Prices were cross-referenced against two independent third-party SG menu-price trackers (sgfoodprice.org, menu-sg.com), which agreed closely on most items. Three items (Black Soya Milk, Ginger Soup Beancurd, Ginger Soup RiceBalls with Beancurd) weren't listed on either tracker — their prices are estimated from the closest comparable item in the same category and flagged via `nutritionNote`. Price is not part of the macro tier system, so this doesn't affect upload-readiness.
